    🔍 HyperGCL: Multi-Modal Graph Contrastive Learning via Learnable Hypergraph Views

     
    📘 IJCNN’25 \ ·\  Hypergraph GCL Adaptive Augmentation
     

    Overview. HyperGCL constructs three complementary hypergraph views from structure and attributes, then applies a learnable topology-aware augmentation. A network-aware contrastive loss aligns modalities while preserving important relations and filtering noise.

     
       
    • Learnable adaptive topology augmentation
      •  
    • Three complementary hypergraph views
      •  
    • Consistent gains over GCL baselines on benchmarks

    🧭 THTN: Topology-Guided Hypergraph Transformer Network

     
    📘 Stanford Graph Learning Workshop’24 · PRICAI’25 \ ·\  Transformers Topology-Aware Attention
     

    Overview. THTN injects structural priors (motifs, centrality) into attention weights to better capture higher-order hypergraph structure, improving classification and robustness.

     
       
    • Guided attention with graph topology
      •  
    • Higher-order structural capture
      •  
    • Improved accuracy across datasets
