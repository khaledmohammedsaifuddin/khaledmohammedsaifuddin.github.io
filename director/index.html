    About

    Dr. Khaled Mohammed Saifuddin works on AI for Science, developing graph machine learning and network science methods for biomedicine, health data, and multimodal reasoning. Before joining Southern Illinois University Carbondale, he was a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Barabási Lab, Northeastern University, under Albert-László Barabási, working on network medicine and drug repurposing in collaboration with Harvard Medical School and Brigham and Women's Hospital. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Georgia State University under Dr. Esra Akbas.

    His research has been published in venues such as ICDE, CIKM, ECML PKDD, IEEE/ACM TCBB, DSAA, ACM-BCB, IJCNN, PRICAI, and IEEE BigData, and presented at the Stanford Graph Learning Workshop and the Broad Institute of MIT & Harvard.
